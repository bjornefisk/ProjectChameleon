# Architecture decision record

```text
ADR ID/title:
Status: proposed | accepted | superseded | rejected
Date/owner/reviewers:

Context/problem:
Authority/contract sections:
Observed evidence/citations:

Decision:
Options considered:
Rejected options/reasons:
Consequences/tradeoffs:
Security/dependency/data impact:
Migration/rollback:
Affected files/callers/docs:
Verification:
Supersedes/superseded by:
Open external facts/owner/recheck date:
```

Accepted ADRs are authoritative. A lower prompt or starter implementation cannot silently reopen or override one.
