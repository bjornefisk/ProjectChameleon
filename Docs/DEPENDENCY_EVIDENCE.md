## Unverified or intentionally deferred

- A clean build and render have not yet been used to prove native-library closure, deterministic PNG bytes, or Apple-Silicon-to-amd64 parity.
- Apt package versions and a complete hash-locked transitive wheelhouse are not yet recorded.
- The selected upstream commit's compatibility with newer ecosystem releases is not assumed; the direct pins intentionally use its checked-in lock baseline.
- Font embedded metadata, glyph coverage, licensing, and redistribution rights are not externally verified here.
- OR-Tools selection, model allowlist/size, exact scoring hardware, and Adobe deployment/backend topology remain outside the ratified Stage 1 contract.
